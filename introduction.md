# PayMe: Fast and Simple Stablecoin Payments

## The Problem: Why It Is Hard to Get Paid Digitally

Today, getting paid for your work is still more difficult than it should be. Traditional financial systems and basic cryptocurrency transfers both have major flaws that frustrate creators and businesses.

If you use traditional platforms like Stripe or PayPal, they take a large cut of your revenue through high transaction fees. Even worse, they often hold your funds for several days before allowing you to withdraw them to your bank account. On the other hand, trying to get paid with standard crypto transfers can feel unprofessional and risky. Expecting a client to copy and paste a long, complex wallet address is a recipe for error. One small mistake in the string can mean money is lost forever, and there is no simple way for you or your client to track the payment on a clean dashboard.

There is a clear gap between the convenience of traditional payment apps and the speed of modern blockchain technology.

**That is why we built PayMe.**

## The Solution: Professional, Instant, and Direct

PayMe bridges the gap. We built a professional payment infrastructure on the Arc Network that feels like a modern fintech app but works with the raw speed and efficiency of stablecoins.

With PayMe, you don't share wallet addresses—you share links. You can create a professional payment link in seconds and share it anywhere: on social media, through email, or in a text message. Your clients pay in USDC or EURC, and the money moves directly from their wallet to yours. Because the platform is non-custodial, PayMe never holds your money. There are no middlemen, no long waiting periods, and no hidden surprises.

## Key Features

### Instant Settlement via Arc
Payments on the Arc Network clear in about one second with deterministic finality. This means your money is verified and ready to spend the moment your client clicks "Confirm," with no risk of the payment being reversed or held in escrow.

### Multi-Token Support (USDC & EURC)
We integrated Circle's premier stablecoins to ensure your payments are global and reliable. Whether you need to get paid in Dollars (USDC) or Euros (EURC), PayMe handles the routing seamlessly, ensuring you receive high-liquidity assets every time.

### Professional Dual-History Dashboard
We included a clean merchant dashboard so you can run your business with clarity. We separated your activity into "Sent" and "Received" tables, allowing you to track exactly who has paid you and manage your own outgoing payments with full on-chain transparency.

### Scan to Pay (QR Codes)
To make mobile checkout effortless, every payment link automatically generates a dedicated QR code. This allows clients viewing links on desktops to scan with their phones and pay instantly through mobile wallets like MetaMask or Coinbase.

### Installable App (PWA) with Notifications
You can install PayMe directly on your phone or desktop home screen without needing an app store. It feels like a native application and includes real-time notifications that alert you the exact second you get paid, even if your browser tab is in the background.

### Secure Expiration Timers
To protect both the buyer and the seller, every payment link is reinforced with a 30-minute expiration timer. This ensures that every transaction is fresh and secure, preventing users from paying into outdated links and keeping your workflow synchronized with the backend.
